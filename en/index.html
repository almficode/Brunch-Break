Where the pause begins

§1

The Brunch Break was born in Arrecife with one obsession: turning brunch into a ritual, not a rush. Every table is a pause; every dish, a reason to stay a little longer.

§2

From the first coffee to the last cake in our display case, everything is thought through and baked with the same care: real ingredients, our own recipes, and a warm space where time moves differently.

Founder & CEO · Creator of concepts, brands and experiences
